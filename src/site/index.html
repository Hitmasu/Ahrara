What, How & Why Ahrara

What is Ahrara?
Disposable email addresses with an inbox on your computer. Read messages in the terminal or browser, or automate with MCP and the local API.
How does it work?
Ahrara generates addresses locally. While it runs, the server streams incoming emails over an encrypted connection to the inbox on your computer.
Why use Ahrara?
Use dedicated addresses to test signups, verification codes and email templates. Keep test emails out of your personal inbox and let your agent handle repetitive checks.

Zero Data Retention

The Ahrara server saves no emails or address records.
Its stateless design keeps delivery simple, with no server inbox to maintain.

FAQ

Does Ahrara store my emails or addresses on its servers?

No. Messages pass through the server without being saved, and email addresses are not registered there. Your inbox and addresses are stored locally on your computer.

Can I send emails with Ahrara?

No. Ahrara only receives emails. It does not support sending messages or replying to them.

Is Ahrara an open email relay?

No. Senders can deliver email without logging in, but Ahrara never relays it to external mail servers. Messages go only to the authenticated Ahrara client for the receiving address.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Set up forwarding from your domain to an Ahrara address, then add the domain in Ahrara. New addresses will use your domain instead of ahrara.dev. See the custom domain guide for setup instructions.
